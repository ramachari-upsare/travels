FAQ

Questions, answered

If something isn’t covered here, call or WhatsApp 9845976916.

How do I book a taxi with Manasvi Tours & Travels?

Call or WhatsApp 9845976916, or fill the booking form on this page. The form prepares a WhatsApp message with your trip details for you to send — we then confirm the vehicle and fare with you on call or WhatsApp.

What is included in the price?

Fares are per kilometre — Etios Rs 15/km, Ertiga Rs 18/km, Innova Crysta Rs 20/km and Tempo Traveller Rs 24/km. Toll, parking and driver allowance are as applicable. The final fare is confirmed on call.

Do you provide airport pick-up and drop?

Yes. Airport pick-up and drop is one of our services. Share your flight timing, pickup point and drop location when you call or send your booking, so the right vehicle is arranged.

Can I book an outstation or all-India trip?

Yes. Outstation trips and all-India service are available. Send your route, travel date, number of passengers and any stops, and we will confirm the vehicle and fare on call or WhatsApp.

How do I pay for the trip?

Payment is arranged directly with us. Please confirm the accepted payment method when you call or message to book — we will tell you what works for your trip.
